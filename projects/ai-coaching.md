## From assessment to action

Unlocking Eve’s [Integrated Leadership Diagnostic](https://www.unlockingeve.org/integrated-leadership-diagnostic) brings together a leader’s self-assessment and the perspective of someone who knows their leadership well. The resulting profile shows where their views align or differ and gives the leader something specific to reflect on.

I helped design an on-demand coach for the moment when a leader wants to apply one of those insights. The conversation starts with a real situation and helps the leader choose a practical action to try. The longer-term vision is to let leaders revisit past commitments and reflect on what changed over time.

## What I worked on

I designed the conversation flow and worked on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Retrieval lets the coach draw on relevant leadership material while responding to the situation the user brings.

For example, before a difficult feedback conversation, a leader might reflect on how they respond under pressure and choose one question to ask before giving advice.
