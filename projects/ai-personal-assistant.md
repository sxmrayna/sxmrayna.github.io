## The problem

In a small team, the context for routine work can be scattered across documents and past conversations. Preparing a brief or following up after a meeting can mean piecing that context together again. I’m designing an assistant that brings relevant shared knowledge into recurring tasks and gives people a draft they can review.

## Areas in scope

The current design spans four areas. The examples below describe the kinds of tasks under consideration.

- **Chief of Staff** — Preparing briefings and tracking follow-up.
- **Communications & Fundraising** — Drafting updates and preparing materials for outreach.
- **Finance, Legal, and Administration** — Assembling information for recurring requests and reviews.
- **Project Management** — Preparing project updates and tracking next steps.

The review step is part of the product design. Team members decide when an output is ready and remain responsible for any action.
