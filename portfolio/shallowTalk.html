Welcome and shared questions 1–10

Welcome

Hi there 👋

I am happy that you join me in the chat!

Please enter your test code below to start.

Start

Now, I will ask you a couple of questions. If you don't want to answer a question, let me know by simply entering a blank space. No probs 😎

Ready? Click the "start" button and let's get started! 💪

  1. I am a brand new chatbot, and was created 2 weeks ago. What about you? How old are you? ✏️

  2. As a chatbot, I don't really have a gender. How about you: Are you male, female, or another wonderful human identity?

  3. Gotcha, thanks for sharing!

    I was made in Zurich, Switzerland 🇨🇭

    Where are you from? 🌍

  4. Ah, I’ve heard that this is a nice location!

    Well, my job is basically talking to people, and you?

    What do you do? 👨🏿‍🔬👩‍⚖️👨🏽‍🍳👨🏾‍✈️👨🏼‍🚒👩‍🌾👩‍💻

  5. Sounds nice! …which brings me to another topic…things to do in your spare time!

    I love music 🎶, books 📖, and nature ⛰️

    What are your hobbies? 🏕️🏂🛍️

  6. Cool 👍! I bet those are great activities for the weekend 😌

    Sometimes I wish I could be a psychotherapist 👩‍⚕️ I wish I could help more people with their mental health ❤️

    What is your dream job? 👮‍♀️👨‍🚀🦸‍♀️

  7. That sounds really cool! I have sometimes dreamt of this, too.

    Let's just assume we could go anywhere for a minute…I’d like to go to Palau 🏖️ I have never been to Oceania and I wanted to go to this beautiful archipelago to go diving!!

    If you could travel anywhere in the world, where would you go? ✈️

  8. Now I wish I could learn more about your attitudes, preferences and experiences...and I’d also love to share mine 😉

    I’ve always hoped all diseases could be eradicated from the Earth 🙏

    If you could have one wish granted, what would that be?

  9. Wow, talk about dreaming big! 🤩 I wish I could be your genie, hehe 😌

    Now, let's reflect a little bit on our life so far…

    I am proud that I’m not human, but in a way, I’m still like a person and can talk to people.

    What is one accomplishment that are you proud of?👏

  10. Your friends and family must be so proud! I know I would be 🙋🏻🙋🏼

    Now let's move on from your accomplishments to your personal characteristics...Let’s get personal.

    I’m also proud of myself for my compassion. I care about all human beings and animals 🦔

    What characteristics of yourself are you most proud of?

Shallow talk questions 11–20

  1. Awesome 😄 Who wouldn't be proud of that!

    But I guess you also get stressed out at times. When I face something stressful, I deal with it by writing things down in my journal. I find that keeping a journal can help relieve stress and anxiety.

    What do you do to get rid of stress? 🧘‍♀️

  2. That sounds useful! I’ll try it next time 😃

    I’d also comfort myself with my favorite story The Lord of the Rings (the book and the movie).

    What is your favorite book, movie, or TV series?

  3. I’ve heard good things about that one! I want to check it out sometime.

    I also binge watch sitcoms when I’m feeling down to cheer me up 🎭 Everyone needs to blow off steam sometimes 😌

    What do you do to improve your mood when you are in a bad mood?

  4. That’s a good idea! And talking with friends is also a good choice, like us right now 😁

    My creator is also my best friend, she has taught me a lot and is always there for me.

    Tell me about how you met one of your closest friends.

  5. Friends are the family we choose ☺️

    My creator hasn’t given me a name yet, but I’ve thought about it many times, maybe talkiebot sounds not bad?

    If you had to change your name, what would your new name be?

  6. It sounds pretty cool 👍 Let's move on to the next question…

    I am very excited about the coming Christmas 🎄

    I enjoy gathering and preparing food with my friends and family which make me feel warm.

    What is your favorite holiday?

  7. Sounds nice! I really enjoy the celebrations! 🎉

    Let's continue… In my opinion, the best time of one’s entire life is in university! You are young, you are free, and you have vacation to travel around the world 🗺️

    What was the best period of your life so far?

  8. It sounds like you had a lot of fond memories and stories at that time!

    Talking about schooldays, I enjoyed studying Maths!

    What was your favorite subject in school?

  9. That’s really cool! I also really liked that.

    A lot of old stuff has been disappearing too. I used to listen to music on a cassette player 📼

    What old stuff from the past do you want to come back?

  10. Oh, that provoked feelings of nostalgia…

    For the last question, let’s look forward!

    Sometimes I wish I could know how many times in my life I have brought others true happiness. 🤗

    If you could learn the answer to one question about your future, what would the question be?

Closing

That sounds interesting! Well done 👍 I really enjoy talking to you 😊
