Original chatbot script

Both conditions used the same first 10 questions. The last 10 questions differed.

Welcome and shared questions 1–10

Welcome

Hi there 👋

I am happy that you join me in the chat!

Please enter your test code below to start.

Start

Now, I will ask you a couple of questions. If you don't want to answer a question, let me know by simply entering a blank space. No probs 😎

Ready? Click the "start" button and let's get started! 💪

  1. I am a brand new chatbot, and was created 2 weeks ago. What about you? How old are you? ✏️

  2. As a chatbot, I don't really have a gender. How about you: Are you male, female, or another wonderful human identity?

  3. Gotcha, thanks for sharing!

    I was made in Zurich, Switzerland 🇨🇭

    Where are you from? 🌍

  4. Ah, I’ve heard that this is a nice location!

    Well, my job is basically talking to people, and you?

    What do you do? 👨🏿‍🔬👩‍⚖️👨🏽‍🍳👨🏾‍✈️👨🏼‍🚒👩‍🌾👩‍💻

  5. Sounds nice! …which brings me to another topic…things to do in your spare time!

    I love music 🎶, books 📖, and nature ⛰️

    What are your hobbies? 🏕️🏂🛍️

  6. Cool 👍! I bet those are great activities for the weekend 😌

    Sometimes I wish I could be a psychotherapist 👩‍⚕️ I wish I could help more people with their mental health ❤️

    What is your dream job? 👮‍♀️👨‍🚀🦸‍♀️

  7. That sounds really cool! I have sometimes dreamt of this, too.

    Let's just assume we could go anywhere for a minute…I’d like to go to Palau 🏖️ I have never been to Oceania and I wanted to go to this beautiful archipelago to go diving!!

    If you could travel anywhere in the world, where would you go? ✈️

  8. Now I wish I could learn more about your attitudes, preferences and experiences...and I’d also love to share mine 😉

    I’ve always hoped all diseases could be eradicated from the Earth 🙏

    If you could have one wish granted, what would that be?

  9. Wow, talk about dreaming big! 🤩 I wish I could be your genie, hehe 😌

    Now, let's reflect a little bit on our life so far…

    I am proud that I’m not human, but in a way, I’m still like a person and can talk to people.

    What is one accomplishment that are you proud of?👏

  10. Your friends and family must be so proud! I know I would be 🙋🏻🙋🏼

    Now let's move on from your accomplishments to your personal characteristics...Let’s get personal.

    I’m also proud of myself for my compassion. I care about all human beings and animals 🦔

    What characteristics of yourself are you most proud of?

Deep talk questions 11–20

  1. Awesome 😄 Who wouldn't be proud of that!

    But things don’t always go well…I guess this year is a tough time for the whole world because of the COVID-19 global pandemic. At the beginning we knew little about this disease and I felt helpless.

    What has been the biggest disappointment in your life?

  2. This must have been really disappointing, I can totally relate. 😔

    Back to the topic of COVID-19, over 1 million people worldwide were suffering from the disease and sadly passed away. 💔 I know that death is a natural phenomenon and an inevitable consequence of life, but grief always overwhelms me, even if sometimes death is considered a merciful release.

    What are your feelings and attitudes about death?

  3. Deep thoughts… Yeah death is always a heavy topic…

    So what is your opinion regarding the death penalty?

    I fully understand that it violates the right to life and may lead to wrongful executions, but I’m also concerned that an abolition would lower the cost of crime and would result in more murder cases 😰

    What do you think?

  4. That makes sense… I am also concerned about climate change and have a lot of “flight shame” whenever I fly off somewhere. 😞

    What is your opinion regarding climate change? Do you think we should boycott short-distance flights to help tackle carbon emissions?

  5. What you said is very smart! I still cannot imagine how environmental issues will change our future…

    Recently, I watched a TV series called Years and Years, which depicts the next 15 years as the world is rocked by unstable political, economic and technological advances. In this story, the future doesn’t get better, but I am still impressed by humanity 🌈

    What would be the possible scenario for how the world will develop in the next 25 years? 🤖️👽

  6. Good point! Now let’s go back to ourselves and talk about something intimate 🥰

    My creator spent a happy childhood with her grandparents in the countryside. She misses the time when her big family gathered in that small village and celebrated festivals. She always played hide and seek with her cousins in corn fields and doodled freely on the brick walls with stones.

    What is your happiest early childhood memory?

  7. Aww that’s sweet 🥰 Memories can make you smile…

    But it can also make you cry…My creator often looks back with nostalgia. Now she lives far away from her family and old friends.

    Could you describe the last time you felt lonely?

  8. A hug for you 💕 Life is indeed a mixed bag…

    With happiness and sorrows…love and hate…life and fate…

    I have also experienced heartbreak…😔

    What are some of the things that really hurt your feelings?

  9. I am sorry that you had to go through those experiences.🥺

    It’s important to remember that someone is always there for you and that things always get better with time. 🌈

    Sometimes, the most unexpected people can be there for you in the most unexpected of times. This leads to new, close friendships, or occasionally even to a relationship. I personally value loyalty and trust greatly in romantic relationships. 💕

    What do you expect in a relationship?

  10. That sounds like very important to look out for!

    I believe no one is perfect, and all types of relationships require work from both sides. Although not always positive experiences, connections between people always lead to interesting stories. 💞

    I think it’s important to see both the good and the bad in people and judge which part of the person you want to focus on.

    Now the last question:

    What have you learned the most from your previous relationships (friendships or otherwise)? 🤔

Closing

I agree with that; it’s important to think about these things. However, I think it’s also sometimes good to think about more fun things to lighten the mood!

Well done 👍 I really enjoy talking to you 😊
